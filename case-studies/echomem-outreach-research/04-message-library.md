# Message Library

这些是结构模板，不应该原样群发。每条消息只使用一个真实 hook。

## 核心产品表达

> Share the useful context, not the whole conversation.

> Your AI conversations stay private. You choose what becomes team context.

> Fewer catch-up meetings. Private-by-default team context.

## 高信号 LinkedIn DM

> Hi {{name}} — “{{short exact hook}}” was the line that caught me.
>
> We’re working on the part between individual AI productivity and team coordination: when someone’s Claude or Codex session contains a useful decision, how can that context reach the right teammate without turning their entire conversation history into company data?
>
> With EchoMem, the conversation stays private. The person chooses one memory to share, and a teammate can later ask their own agent about it—with the owner and original source attached. We think this could replace some of the catch-up meetings and stakeholder check-ins that exist only to move context around.
>
> The first version is working. We’re looking for a few teams to try one small workflow with us, and we’d handle the setup. Would you be open to testing it with 3–5 people?

## 公开 comment：Privacy

> The privacy boundary seems like the hard part here. If every person’s agent has its own context, how do you let useful decisions travel across the team without turning everyone’s full AI history into shared company data?

## 公开 comment：Meeting 与 check-in

> I wonder if some of the productivity gain comes back as coordination work. Are teams saving time on execution, then spending more time in check-ins to keep everyone aligned?

## 公开 comment：Ownership

> The interesting question may not be how to give agents more context, but who gets to decide which context becomes company knowledge.

## Pilot offer

> We’d keep it small: 3–5 people, one workflow, 4–6 weeks. We’ll handle setup, nothing is shared unless a member chooses it, and the team can export or delete everything. Before starting, we’ll agree on one outcome—fewer context-transfer meetings, less duplicated work, or faster stakeholder answers.
